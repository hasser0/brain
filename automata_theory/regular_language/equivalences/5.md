## Transitive closure method

Given a deterministic automata $A$ we can compute the values
$$
R_{ij}^{(k)} = \begin{cases}
\varepsilon & k=0,i=j,\text{ no loop}  \\
\varepsilon+a+b+c+\cdots & k=0,i=j,\text{ loops}  \\
\varnothing & k=0,i\neq j\text{ no transition} \\
a+b+c+\cdots& k=0,i\neq j\text{ transition} \\
R_{ij}^{(k-1)} + R_{ik}^{(k-1)} R_{kk}^{(k-1)}R_{kj}^{(k-1)}  & k\neq 0,\text{ transition} \\
\end{cases}
$$
which defines the regular expression to move from state $i$ to state $j$ passing
through states with a value equal or less than $k$. This values are computed
recursively using the previous formulas to finally get the general regular
expression as
$$
R=\sum_{f\in F} R_{0f}^{(n)}
$$
where $n$ is the number of states and $F$ is the set of final states
