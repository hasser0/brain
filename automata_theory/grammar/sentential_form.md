## Sentential form

Given a grammar $G$ any string consisting of terminals and non terminals
$\alpha$ is sentential form iff $S\Rightarrow \alpha$. Although any string
$w\in L(G)$ is a sentential form, not all sentential forms are strings of the
language, because $w$ is a sequence of terminals, and a sentential form can
contain non terminals.
