## Final state language is empty stack language

Given a pushdown automata $S=\langle Q,\Sigma,\Gamma,\delta_S,q_0,Z_0,F\rangle$
its final state language $L_S$ could be generated by the empty stack language of
the pushdown automata
$$
N=\langle Q\cup\{p_0,p_f\},\Sigma,\Gamma\cup\{X_0\},\delta_N,p_0,X_0\rangle
$$
such that

1. $\delta_N(p_0,\varepsilon,X_0)=\{(q_0,Z_0X_0)\}$
2. $\forall p\in F,\alpha\in\Gamma\quad(p_f,\varepsilon)\in\delta_N(p,\varepsilon,\alpha)$
3. $\forall \alpha\in\Gamma\quad(p_f,\varepsilon)\in\delta_N(p_f,\varepsilon,\alpha)$
4. $\delta_S\subset\delta_N$
