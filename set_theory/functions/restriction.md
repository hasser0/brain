## Restriction

Let R be any relation and $A$ any set, then the restriction of $R$ under $A$ is
defined as
$$
R|A = \{\langle x, y\rangle: xRy,\ x\in A\}
$$

Generally the restriction is defined under relations, but it is usually found in
the context of functions
