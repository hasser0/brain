### Inverse

Let $R$ be any relation, then the inverse of $R$ denoted as $R^{-1}$ is
$$
R^{-1} = \{\langle y, x\rangle: xRy\}
$$

Generally the inverse is defined under relations, but it is usually found in
the context of functions
