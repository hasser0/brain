## Composition

Let $R, S$ be relations, then we define $R\circ S$ as
$$
R\circ S = \{\langle x, y\rangle:\exists t(xSt,\ tRy)\}
$$

Generally the composition is defined under relations, but it is usually found in
the context of functions
