## Infinite cartesian product

Infinite cartesian product is conveniently defined using functions instead of
ordered pairs as
$$
\textsf{X}_{i\in I} H(i) = \{f| \text{dom }f = I\text{ and } \forall i\in I: f(i)\in H(i)\}
$$
