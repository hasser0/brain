## Relations

A relation $R$ is a set of exclusivelly ordered pairs

$\text{dom}\ R = \{x\in \bigcup\bigcup R: \exists t\ \langle x,t\rangle\in R\}$

$\text{ran}\ R = \{x\in \bigcup\bigcup R: \exists t\ \langle t,x\rangle\in R\}$

$\text{fld}\ R = \text{dom}\ R\ \cup\ \text{ran}\ R$
