## Cartesian product

Define the cartesian product for a pair of sets $A, B$ as
$$
A\times B = \{\langle x, y\rangle\in \mathcal{P}\mathcal{P}(A\cup B) : x\in A, y\in B\}
$$

Note that if $x, y\in C$ then $\langle x, y\rangle\in \mathcal{P}\mathcal{P} C$
