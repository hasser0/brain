## Induction principle

Any inductive subset $A$ of $\omega$ is $\omega$. We know $A$ is inductive,
therefore $\omega\subseteq A$ and by hypothesis $A\subseteq \omega$. If we can
prove that a set $X$ contains $\varnothing$ and that $X$ is inductive, then $X$
is $\omega$. This is the induction principle.
