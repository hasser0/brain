## Myhill Nerode relations

An equivalence relation $\equiv$ is said to be a Myhill Nerode over a set
$A\subseteq \Sigma^*$ iff

1. $x,y\in\Sigma^*,a\in \Sigma$: $x\equiv y$ then $xa\equiv ya$
2. $x\equiv y$ implies $x\in A \Leftrightarrow y\in A$
3. the number of equivalence relations is finite
