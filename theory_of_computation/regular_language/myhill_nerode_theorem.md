## Myhill Nerode theorem

Myhill Nerode relation states that the following three are equivalent statements

1. A set $A$ is regular
2. Exists a MN relation for $A$
3. The relation $\equiv_A$ is of finite index

It is helpful for the proof to show that both constructions from DFA to MN and
its inverse are isomorphisms
