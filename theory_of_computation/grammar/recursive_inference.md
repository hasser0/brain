## Recursive inference

Given a string $w$ and a grammar $G$ recursive inference is one of the two methods to
proof that $w$ is generated by the grammar $G$.

Recursive inference, similar to derivation, connects $S$ with $w$ using two principles

1. A base production rule of the form $S\rightarrow w$ where $w\in T^*$ as
   hypothesis
2. If $S\rightarrow X_1\dots X_n$ and $w_i\in X_i$ then $w_i\dots w_n\in G$
