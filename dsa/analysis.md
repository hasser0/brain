## RAM model of computation

+ Worst case
+ Best case
+ Average case
+ Input size
+ Running time

Big Oh
$$
f(n) = O(g(n)) \rightarrow \forall n>n_0: f(n) \le c \cdot g(n)
$$

Omega
$$
f(n) = \Omega(g(n)) \rightarrow \forall n>n_0: f(n) \ge c \cdot g(n)
$$

Theta
$$
f(n) = \Theta(g(n)) \rightarrow \forall n>n_0: c_1 \cdot g(n) \le f(n) \le c_2 \cdot g(n) \\
$$
