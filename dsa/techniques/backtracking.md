## Backtracking

Backtracking is an special case of recursion that solves a subproblem by finding
the best decision among all current possibilities and whenever no solution is
possible for the path, backtrack and tries a different path. Backtracking is a
refinement of brute force that avoid searching the entire space by using a
smarter approach
