## Proof

A proof in a formal theory is a finite sequence of wff
$\langle w_1,\dots,w_n \rangle$ such that for any wff in the sequence

1. $w_i$ is an axiom of the formal theory
2. $w_i$ is a direct consequence of a subset of the previous wffs
   $\{w_1,\dots,w_{i-1}\}$
