## RAM

Volatile memory or RAM(random access memory) lose information without energy.
Its name came from its capacity to access any memory location at the same speed,
in contrast with its predecessors that need sequential access and were slower.

The most common implementations of RAM are shown in the following table with its
trade offs.


| Technology | Transistors | Latency |
|------------|-------------|---------|
| Flip flop  | ~20         | Fast    |
| SRAM       | ~6          | Medium  |
| DRAM       | 1           | Slow    |

The number of transistor is correlated with its cost and size.
