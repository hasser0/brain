## Subset construction

Given a nondeterministic automata $A=\{Q,\Sigma,q_0,\delta,F\}$
its transition function $\delta$ could be understood as $\delta:\mathcal{P}Q
\times \Sigma \mapsto \mathcal{P}Q$

We can construct a deterministic automata
$A^*=\{Q^*,\Sigma,\{q_0\},\delta,F^*\}$
where $Q^* = \{x|x\in\mathcal{P}Q\}$ and
$F^* = \{x|x\in\mathcal{P}Q,\ x\cap F \neq \varnothing \}$
