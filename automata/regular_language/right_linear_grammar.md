## Right linear grammar

A grammar $G=\langle T, V, P, S\rangle$ is right linear iff every production
rule is of the form $A\rightarrow sB$ or $A\rightarrow B$ where $A, B$ are non
terminals and $s$ is terminal.

Each regular language has a right linear grammar and each right linear grammar
represents a regular language
