## DFA construction from regex

To show that each regex has a corresponding DFA we prove it inductively. For the
base cases $\varepsilon,0,1,a,b,c,\dots$ creating a DFA is fairly simple.

For the induction step remember that regex are generated by

1. Concatenation
2. Addition
3. Closure

Given $\alpha, \beta$ regex and their DFAs $M_\alpha, M_\beta$ we can construct
an automata for each of the operations; therefore all regex has a DFA.
