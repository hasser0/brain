## Homomorphism

Define $h:\Sigma^*\mapsto \Gamma^*$ a function such that $h(w)=h(w_1)\cdots h(w_n)$
where $w\in\Sigma^*$.

Let $A\subseteq \Sigma^*$ and $B\subseteq \Gamma^*$, then define
$$
h(A)=\{h(x): x\in A\}\subseteq \Gamma^*
$$
and
$$
h^{-1}(B)=\{x: h(x)\in B\}\subseteq \Sigma^*
$$
