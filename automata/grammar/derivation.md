## Derivation

Given a string $w$ and a grammar $G$ derivation is one of the two methods to
proof that $w$ is generated by the grammar $G$.

Starting from the initial symbol $S$ and using the production rules $P$ find a
path $S\Rightarrow \gamma_1\Rightarrow\cdots\Rightarrow\gamma_n\Rightarrow w$
that connects $S$ with $w$
