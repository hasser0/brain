## Empty stack language is final state language

Given a pushdown automata $N=\langle Q,\Sigma,\Gamma,\delta_N,q_0,Z_0\rangle$
its empty stack language $L_N$ could be generated by a final state language of
the pushdown automata
$$
S=\langle Q\cup\{p_0,p_f\},\Sigma,\Gamma\cup\{X_0\},\delta_S,p_0,X_0,\{p_f\}\rangle
$$
such that

1. $\delta_S(p_0,\varepsilon,X_0)=\{(q_0,Z_0X_0)\}$
2. $\delta_N\subset\delta_S$
3. $\forall p\in Q\quad(p_f,\varepsilon)\in\delta_S(p,\varepsilon,X_0)$
