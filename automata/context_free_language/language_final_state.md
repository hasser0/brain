## By final state

Given a pushdown automata $G=\langle Q,\Sigma,\Gamma,\delta_N,q_0,Z_0,F\rangle$,
its language by final state is
$$
\{w|(q_0,w,Z_0)\vdash(p,\varepsilon,\alpha)\}
$$
where $p\in F$
