## Contamination time

Contamination time, for combinational or sequential circuits, is the amount of
time for an input signal to change any output signal, whether that is the
correct or incorrect result

Contamination delay for a combinational circuit is from input to output

Clock to Q contamination for a register is from clock's rising edge to the
output
