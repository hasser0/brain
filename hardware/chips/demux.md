## Demultiplexor

The demultiplexor is a chip with inputs $a$, $s$ and outputs $x_0,x_1$ such that

+ when $s=1$ then $x_0=a,x_1=0$
+ when $s=0$ then $x_0=0,x_1=a$

Generalization for arrays $x[2^n]$ and $s[n]$ are similar
