## Synchronizer

A synchronizer is a circuit that receives an asynchronous signal and either
outputs low or high with high probability. When the synchronizer outputs another
value it is said to fail; a synchronizer could fail, but it is designed to
reduce the probability of failure.

Synchronizer is used to avoid metastability in sequential circuits
