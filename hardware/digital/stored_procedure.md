## Stored procedure

Stored procedure is the basic idea behind general computer. Before stored
procedures, machines were purpose specific and not capable of changing its
capabilities, the working mechanisms and the material sustrate were tightly
couple.

A computer capable of changing its behaviour is the same idea behind a universal
Turing machine, where the Turing machine to be emulated is encoded as input. In
the same way, the stored procedure are the instructions that the general purpose
machine runs
