## Logic families

Logic families are groups of electrical gates with specific the rules that
interprete analog voltage as digits. They define

+ Positive supply voltage
+ Input/output low voltage(0 signal upper bound)
+ Input/output high voltage(1 signal lower bound)

For each logic family, there are different parameters to evaluate while creating
digital gates such as

+ Speed
+ Size
+ Design time
+ Cost
+ Energy efficiency

Some digital gates are better in the previous terms than others depending on the
logic family
