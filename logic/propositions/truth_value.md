## Truth value

In general, for any logic, the truth value is a value assigned to a proposition,
either simple or compose that determines a relation with the real world

In classical logic, a sentence or proposition can only have one of two truth
values; **true** or **false**. For simple proposition this value is derived
directly from experience or taken for granted, while composite proposition
truth value's are calculated recursively depending on the connector of the top
most sentences.
