## Direct consequence

Given wffs $\gamma_1,\dots,\gamma_n,\alpha$ in a formal theory with inference
rules $R_1,\dots R_p$ it is said that $\alpha$ is a direct consequence of
$\{\gamma_1,\dots,\gamma_n\}$ by inference rule $R_i$ iff
$$
\langle\gamma_1,\dots,\gamma_n,\alpha\rangle\in R_i
$$
for some $R_i$ of the formal theory
