## Ordered pairs

$$
\langle x, y\rangle = \{\{ x\}, \{ x, y\}\}
$$

To define order pairs as sets we need to encode the ordering. Several options
exists, but the standard is the Kuratowski encoding, defined above.

It can be proved that $\langle x, y\rangle = \langle u, v\rangle$ iff $x = u$
and $y = u$.
